# Macro Framework

## Summary
What are the current major narratives/themes driving the market skew? Is the narrative sustainable (look at key drivers) — if not, when and to what extent? What is the next narrative and why?

People underestimate events occurring if they do not personally see them often, and vice versa.

Whenever the market is pointing at something and saying this is a risk to be concerned about, most of the time the risk ends up being not as bad as the market anticipated. The opposite also holds true — if the market does not talk about the risk.

You can follow the lead-up using "buy the rumor, sell the fact."

Range-bound is also a trend — focus on mean reversions.

1. **Inflationary Bust** — Buy: Cash in reserve currencies / Sell: Financial assets
2. **Disinflationary Bust** — Buy: Bonds / Sell: Everything else

### Which Category Is It Under? (Monetary Policy vs. Growth)

1. **Slowing Growth with Tightening** — Buy: Defensive assets (DXY, Cash, VIX, Puts on risk assets) / Sell: Non-risk assets
2. **Slowing Growth with Easing** — Buy: FI, Growth stocks, DM equities / Sell: High-beta, real economy assets (EM, Value, Cyclicals, Commodities)
3. **Strong Growth with Tightening** — Buy: High-beta, real economy assets (EM, Value, Cyclicals, Industrial) / Sell: Higher-rates-vulnerable assets (FI, TIPS, Gold, Growth)
4. **Strong Growth with Easing** — Buy: Everything / Sell: DXY, Cash, Volatility

## Positioning and Flows

### What Is the Current Positioning/Flows?

Positioning and sentiment tend to be trend indicators; only fade if positioning is extreme.

Put on fades when:
1. A new theme/event passing will unwind positioning
2. Stop-loss runs when price starts to move against heavy positioning

Look at: COT, Put/Call ratio (if low in a rising market, it signals investors are so bullish they stop hedging).

### What Is the Current Sentiment?

Investor Flows, Fear and Greed, AAII Survey (if investors are bullish with market prices falling, consider fading), DSI.

If bad/good news does not lead to a fall/rise in the market, then it is strong market sentiment — follow it.

If bad/good news does not lead to a fall/rise in the market for the asset-class-related news, then it is strong market sentiment. Look at the correct asset class reaction price and highlight these.
